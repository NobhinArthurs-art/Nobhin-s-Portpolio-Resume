About

Grounded in hardware, curious about intelligence.

I'm a motivated, detail-oriented B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, with hands-on experience across IoT, machine learning, Python, Arduino, and software engineering.

I like building things that sense the real world — soil moisture, patient vitals, battery heat — and pipe that data somewhere useful, using protocols like MQTT and CoAP and dashboards like ThingSpeak. On the software side, I care about writing structured, well-documented code, from Python data tools to SQLite-backed desktop apps.

I'm currently deepening my skills in AI/ML fundamentals and cloud technologies, and looking for opportunities to apply IoT and machine learning to real, practical problems.

How I Build

Sense. Analyze. Act.

The loop behind every project on this page — from a soil sensor to a hospital monitor.

01 — SENSE

Capture the real world

Arduino UNO R4 WiFi and sensors — DHT11, PIR, ultrasonic, soil moisture — pull raw signal from the physical environment.

02 — TRANSMIT & ANALYZE

Move and make sense of data

MQTT and CoAP carry readings to ThingSpeak; Python, Pandas, and NumPy turn raw streams into trends worth acting on.

03 — ACT

Close the loop

LED alerts, cloud dashboards, and well-documented software put the insight back in front of the person who needs it.
